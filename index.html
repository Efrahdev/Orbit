Library

Sync across devices, back up everything, or bring in Chrome folders.

Google Drive syncOff. Bookmarks are saved only on this device.
First device: set up in 2 minutes (no Google Cloud needed)
  1. It contains your own secret key.
  2. Open script.google.com (a new project opens). Select all the sample code, then paste.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment. Click the gear icon next to “Select type” → Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Then click Deploy.
  4. Click Authorise access and choose your account. Google warns that the app is unverified because it is your own script: click Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow.
  5. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec) and paste it below.
Google Drive sync is private. Your library is kept as a file in your own Google Drive. Only your own script can reach it, and only with your secret key. Without sync, bookmarks stay on this device: transfer a JSON backup instead. A published snapshot is visible to anyone who can access the site. Never publish private or work-confidential links.
